Our Business & Non-Profit Goals

Peaks Prehab is built as a non-profit resource hub to serve our community.

Social Mission

Based on Sarah's original notes, Peaks Prehab operates with a clear structure:

  • Non-Profit Resource Hub: All core educational materials, articles, and guidelines are offered completely free of charge to any visitor.
  • Low-Cost Hosting & Operations: We prioritize affordable technology solutions to keep our overhead low and direct support programs fully active.
  • One-Stop Shop for Recovery: By coordinating mental health, physical fitness, nutrition, and spirituality tips in one single place, we reduce the burden of navigation for clients.
