Fitness & Movement

Smart movement is the base of all prehab. Build strength, flexibility, and stability the right way.

Getting Started with Prehab Movement

  • Start Low, Go Slow

    Begin with 10–15 minute walks each day. Gradual movement is safer and more sustainable than sudden intense exercise.

  • Focus on Stability

    Core stability exercises (bird-dog, dead bug, bridges) are the foundation of prehab fitness. These protect your joints.

  • Daily Mobility Work

    Spend 5 minutes each morning doing simple joint mobility drills — ankles, hips, and shoulders. This prevents stiffness.
