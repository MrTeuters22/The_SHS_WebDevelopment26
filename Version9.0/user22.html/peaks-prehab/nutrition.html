Nutrition & Recovery

Building your physical base starts with what you feed your body.

Key Nutrition Tips for Recovery

Prehab and recovery nutrition focus on anti-inflammatory whole foods, proper hydration, and maintaining muscle mass. Here are the core pillars we recommend starting with:

  • Hydration is Key

    Drink half of your body weight in ounces of water daily to support tissue repair and prevent fatigue.

  • Prioritize Clean Protein

    Adequate protein intake supports muscle protein synthesis. Focus on lean poultry, fish, eggs, beans, and nuts.

  • Anti-inflammatory Foods

    Include leafy greens, berries, olive oil, and turmeric in your daily diet to help combat joint inflammation.
