About Sarah Oelfe

Peaks Prehab was founded by Sarah Oelfe with a simple mission: to focus on prevention, education, and balance, not just treatment. As a guide in our clients' wellness journeys, we provide the tools, support, and encouragement needed to stay healthy, confident, and pain-free.

Whether you're recovering from injury, preparing for surgery (prehab), or working on general wellness, we offer custom coaching, resource libraries, and peer support to help you reach your goals.
