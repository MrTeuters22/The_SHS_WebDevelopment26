Mental Health & Mindfulness

A healthy mind is the foundation of a strong body.

Strategies for Mental Resilience

Prehabilitation is as much mental as it is physical. Dealing with injuries, recovery plans, or surgeries takes psychological strength. We recommend focusing on the following areas:

  • Daily Mindfulness

    Spend 5-10 minutes each morning in quiet reflection or breathing exercises to center your focus and reduce stress hormones.

  • Build Your Support Network

    Talk openly with friends, family, or support groups about your challenges. Keeping stress bottled up slows recovery.

  • Set Small, Achievable Goals

    Break down your rehabilitation journey into small milestones. Celebrate every step forward to build momentum.
